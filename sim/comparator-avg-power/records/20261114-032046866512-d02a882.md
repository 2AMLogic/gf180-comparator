- **Note**: SIGN CONVENTION, AND THE ONE PLACE abs() IS ALLOWED. ngspice reports i(vsupa) as the current into the source's positive terminal, i.e. negative while the source delivers. Every charge, energy and power quantity here is built from i_DD = -i(vsupa), so it is positive for delivered energy. DR-0006 forbids clamping the window integral with abs(): a negative q_win_pc is invalid, and its min-0 floor is what makes that so. abs() appears in exactly three places, none of them the window integral: the half-window |difference| of the settledness check (which DR-0006 permits explicitly), the form-agreement |difference| of p_avg_form_delta_pct, and the instantaneous magnitude of the irst reset-activity probe. This is a deliberate departure from sim/comparator-regeneration/'s e_dec_fj = abs(q_dec - i_stat*8.1e-9)*..., which also subtracts a hard-coded static term; no static subtraction is applied here, because DR-0006's P_avg is the TOTAL delivered supply power.
- **Note**: THE ACCOUNTING BOUNDARY IS THE DUT'S OWN SUPPLY SOURCE. One DUT instance hangs on vsupa and nothing else does, and the bias reference current is drawn from that same rail (iba vdda ibn), which is DR-0006's boundary verbatim -- it counts the bias current's charge and forbids a separate ibias supply. The 20 fF output loads recharge through the DUT's output stages from the same rail, so their energy is counted exactly once, in the supply charge, and never added again as a C*V^2 term. The ideal clock, input and common-mode sources are referenced to ground and are outside the boundary; q_clk_pc records the clock source's own charge as the separate informational line DR-0006 asks for, and nothing derives power from it. How much the figure MOVES if that boundary or the 20 fF load is changed is DR-0006's sensitivity list and is not run here.
- **Note**: WHY THE WINDOW IS WHERE IT IS. Cycle 0's rising edge is at t = 10 ns (the instant sim/comparator-regeneration/'s deck also uses). The first 8 cycles (500 ns) are warm-up and are discarded, so the window runs from the rising edge at 510 ns to the rising edge at 1510 ns: N = 16 whole cycles, 1.000 us. N is even, so the window holds a whole number (8) of the two-cycle input-polarity period -- without that, alternating-polarity charge would not average out over the window and the two halves could not be compared. Both ends are anchored at the START of a rising edge, so the interval is an exact integer number of periods however the edge is anchored. The warm-up and window lengths are DR-0006's PROPOSED numbers, and item 3 of its feasibility list is precisely that they are not yet shown adequate: q_half_mismatch_pct is the measurement that tests them.
- **Note**: THE 20 ns SETUP TIME IS DR-0006'S INPUT-TIMING CLAUSE, NOT A FREE CHOICE. The stimulus pulse is shaped so that every polarity transition COMPLETES exactly 20 ns before the next rising edge and BEGINS 11.15 ns after the preceding falling edge -- inside the reset phase, as the record requires, so no decision is taken on a transient. sim/comparator-regeneration/'s own settling argument (">10 time constants" for the front-end output pole at a 20 ns flip) is what that number is inherited from.
- **Note**: RESET COMPLETION IS MEASURED ON THE SUPPLY CURRENT, NOT ON AN INTERNAL NODE, AND THAT IS A REAL LIMITATION. In this topology (DR-0001: StrongARM latch, isolation inverters, NOR SR output latch) the SR latch HOLDS dout through the reset phase by design, so no observable output node resets; what resets is the StrongARM's internal precharge nodes, whose names exist only in the schematic hierarchy and not in a flat extracted netlist, so probing them would make the bench unusable against the extracted binding the 45-corner follow-up needs. reset_margin_ns is therefore defined on the DUT's supply current: the time left in the last window cycle's reset phase after the last instant at which the current magnitude exceeded 110 % of the SAME RUN's quiescent value (measured over the final 2.9 ns of that phase). It is a 10 %-settling margin for the precharge transient, which is a PROXY for reset completion and not a direct measurement of the latch's internal state; what directly establishes that reset completed is that all 16 window cycles decided correctly on alternating inputs (dec_cycles_correct). The quantity carries NO CHECK: DR-0006 states no bound on it and inventing one here would be a bound the record never proposed.
- **Note**: SOLVER TOLERANCES ARE sim/comparator-regeneration/'s, DELIBERATELY. reltol = 1e-4 and vntol = 1e-9 are tightened from the ngspice defaults so a charge integral over 16 cycles is resolved rather than smeared. abstol is 1e-13 and NOT 1e-15 for the reason that deck records: the PWL/pulse-driven controlled sources present branch currents far below a 1e-15 floor at t = 0, and six of the 45 points of that grid failed to take their first transient step at all. A re-run that changes any of the three is not bit-comparable with a record minted under them.
- **Note**: THE OFFSET PROBE RUNS ONLY AGAINST AN EXTRACTED DUT (issue #23), and it is declared here because DR-0006 defines the overdrive as "+/- Vod above/below the DUT's own trip point (schematic: trip point 0 V; extracted: the per-corner probed dut_vos, as the existing tb_vosprobe.spice flow does)". Against the schematic binding the probe does not run, dut_vos is 0, and the stimulus is +/- v_od about 0 V. The probe's clock is this bench's 16 MHz / 50 % clock rather than the regeneration deck's 30 ns rig, so the trip point is located under the same clocking as the measurement it refers; the ramp moves (2*vspan)/tramp = 1 V/ms and the decision updates once per 62.5 ns period, so the trip point is resolved to about 62.5 uV -- 160x finer than the 10 mV overdrive it refers. No extracted record is minted by this issue.
