- **Note**: TWO SOURCE IMPEDANCES, BOTH IN THE RECORD, AND NEITHER IS AN IDEAL VOLTAGE SOURCE. An ideal source restores the injected charge instantly and reports ~0 kickback however bad it is -- the standard way this measurement is faked. Instance A uses the 1 kohm impedance README.md's kickback row is stated at (peak disturbance); instances B and C use a 1 pF node through 1 Gohm, RC = 1 ms against a ~30 ns cycle, so the bias sets the operating point and then does nothing (residual charge).
- **Note**: ONLY THE SIGNAL-DEPENDENT PART IS IRREDUCIBLE. A residue-independent kick is indistinguishable from comparator offset and is removed the same way; that is why B and C sit at 1 mV and 100 mV residues and why their difference (kick_sigdep_nv) is called out separately from the absolute residual.
- **Note**: THE RESOLUTION FLOOR IS A PROPERTY OF THE MEASUREMENT AND IT IS IN THE TABLE. ngspice's `meas` returns ~6 significant digits, so differencing two ~1.2 V node levels quantizes the answer at ~1 uV. kick_resid_raw_uv does exactly that and shows the floor; kick_resid_small_nv measures the same physical quantity through a probe referenced near 0 V and resolves it ~1000x finer. The sibling repo's own kickback record documents this floor and states that beating it requires measuring an already-small quantity rather than tightening tolerances -- this bench acts on that finding rather than restating it.
- **Note**: MISMATCH IS OFF (PDK default sw_stat_mismatch = 0). Kickback and offset are separable and are budgeted separately; a mismatched instance would add its offset to the measured displacement and make neither attributable.
- **Note**: THE INPUT NODE IS A LUMPED CAPACITANCE, not a modelled driving network. There is no CDAC, no sampling switch and no sequencer here -- that is the point of a standalone bench. The lumped model is conservative for the residual kick (no path to restore charge at all) and optimistic for the peak (no distributed series resistance), which is why the peak is reported separately rather than folded into one number.
- **Note**: PLACEHOLDER CAVEAT. While sim/dut.json binds the placeholder DUT, the coupling from its decision stage back to the front end is an explicit 5 fF/side stand-in for a real latch input pair's C_gd (declared in sim/dut/placeholder_comparator.spice), not an extracted capacitance. The kickback numbers here therefore exercise the measurement path end to end but do not characterise any real topology.
- **Note**: OFFSET-CENTRED DRIVES FOR EXTRACTED DUTS (issue #23). A flat extracted netlist carries the layout's own deterministic input-referred offset (several mV on this layout, from per-net parasitic asymmetry between the two sides), which moves the comparator's trip point away from 0 V differential. The '1 mV residue' rung only probes the near-threshold regime if the drive sits 1 mV above the ACTUAL trip point, so against an extracted binding the harness probes the trip point first (tb_vosprobe.spice, per PVT point) and centres the three drive sources on it via the dut_vos param. The kickback disturbance measurements themselves (ad/bd/cd-referenced) are unaffected by the centring. Against the schematic binding the probe does not run and dut_vos is 0, leaving the schematic measurement byte-equivalent to the pre-#23 deck.
