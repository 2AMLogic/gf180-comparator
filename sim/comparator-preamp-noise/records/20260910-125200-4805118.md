- **Note**: TOTAL INTEGRATED NOISE, NOT A DENSITY. A comparator samples its input noise at the decision instant rather than filtering it, so the band-limited total is the relevant quantity. That is why the integration runs to 1 GHz rather than to some assumed signal bandwidth.
- **Note**: THE DECISION STAGE IS INSTANTIATED IN RESET so that the front end's noise bandwidth is set by the capacitance it actually drives. Omitting it would over-state the bandwidth and therefore over-state the noise; inventing a lumped capacitor instead would make the answer depend on the invention.
- **Note**: MISMATCH IS OFF (PDK default sw_stat_mismatch = 0). Noise and offset are separate terms budgeted separately; a mismatched instance would shift the operating point the small-signal analysis is taken about and make neither number attributable.
- **Note**: fnoicor IS AT THE PDK DEFAULT (0, as-extracted). Every flicker number in this repo must state its fnoicor setting; the flicker_frac_pct column is what lets a reader rescale the flicker part for the worst-case setting and see immediately whether it would change a verdict.
- **Note**: WHAT IS NOT IN THIS RECORD: the decision stage's own noise. `.noise` cannot reach a reset-and-regenerate structure at all (no DC operating point), and ngspice offers no PSS/pnoise. Referred to the comparator input that term is divided by the gain measured here, which is why the front end is where the budget is closed.
